Aurora Asset Editor is not signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time.

1. Drag Aurora Asset Editor to Applications and open it. macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious software.
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm.
   On macOS 14 or earlier you can instead right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Or run this in Terminal:
xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Aurora Asset Editor.app"
